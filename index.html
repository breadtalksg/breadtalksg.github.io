Ryan Moomoo Sheet Sync

Privately operated Google Drive and Google Sheets integration

Ryan Moomoo Sheet Sync is a personal-use application for organising investment portfolio and transaction records, supporting data synchronisation and analysis through Google Drive and Google Sheets.

How it works

The application connects to a Google account only after that account's owner explicitly authorises the requested Google permissions. Its functions may include reading designated files, updating designated spreadsheet records, and maintaining synchronisation records as required by the operator's setup.

The application is not a public financial service and does not execute brokerage trades. Google OAuth access is separate from brokerage credentials or trading authorisations.

Access and control

Google account owners may review or revoke access at any time in their Google Account connections. Revocation can interrupt synchronisation until access is authorised again.
