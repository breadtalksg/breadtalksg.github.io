Privacy Policy

Application: Ryan Moomoo Sheet Sync
Effective date: 19 November 2026
Contact: luoyujia@gmail.com

Purpose

This privately operated application is designed to organise and synchronise investment records using Google Drive and Google Sheets for personal portfolio analysis.

Google user data accessed

Depending on the permissions granted during Google sign-in and the operator's integration configuration, the application may access Google Drive files and folders, Google Sheets data, and basic Google account identity information needed to establish an authorised connection. The precise permissions requested are displayed on Google's consent screen.

Use of data

Google data is used to locate, read, create or update designated portfolio files, spreadsheets and synchronisation information, only within the permissions granted to the application. The application does not use Google data to place brokerage trades.

Storage and retention

Relevant synchronisation records and portfolio files may remain in the authorised Google Drive or Sheets account. Local application configuration and OAuth credentials may be stored by the operator's integration environment to maintain the connection. Retention and deletion of those local items depend on the operator's configured system and backup policies. Do not enter Google passwords or OAuth secrets into the public website.

Sharing and disclosure

The application is intended for the operator's personal use rather than distribution as a public service. No advertising or sale of Google user data is part of its intended purpose. Information may be processed by Google to provide Google Drive, Sheets, and OAuth services. Any additional processors or sharing arrangements must be disclosed here if the deployed integration uses them.

Security

Access requires Google OAuth authorisation. Application credentials should be protected from public disclosure, and access should follow the least-privilege principle. Actual security controls depend on the deployed integration and should be reviewed by its operator.

User choices and deletion

Users can revoke the application's Google access at Google Account connections. To request removal of integration-specific data stored outside the Google account, contact luoyujia@gmail.com. Google Drive or Sheets files remain subject to their owner's Google account controls and retention settings.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's data usage, permissions, or storage practices change. The current version will be posted here.
